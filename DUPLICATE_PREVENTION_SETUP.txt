FAMILYGUARD ONE-RESPONSE RESTRICTION — REQUIRED SETUP

This version applies the same restriction to the standard and Freelancer contest links.

1. In Cloudflare D1 > familyguard-survey-db > Console, run duplicate-participation-migration.sql once. It creates the survey_ip_participation table and does not delete existing data.
2. In Cloudflare Pages > your project > Settings > Variables and Secrets, add a SECRET named DUPLICATE_HASH_SECRET. Use a random secret at least 32 characters long. Keep it private and out of Git.
3. Redeploy after adding the secret and migration.
4. Verify /api/participation-status returns participated:false before a response. After a completed response, both links must show “You’ve already participated.”

Privacy/accuracy note: the site stores a salted one-way hash, not the raw IP. IP addresses are not unique people: shared networks may block multiple family members, and VPN/network changes can bypass the restriction. The survey consent text discloses the check.

Do not distribute the public link until the migration and secret are configured. If setup is incomplete, the survey intentionally remains unavailable.
